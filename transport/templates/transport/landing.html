About CampusRide

Streamlining College Transit Operations

CampusRide is a centralized digital transportation platform designed for colleges to manage buses, routes, student registrations, bus pass approvals, and real-time seat capacity.

Zero Paperwork

Instant online bus pass applications and auto-generated digital passes complete with QR details.

Capacity Management

Automatic seat allocation prevents bus overcrowding and ensures every student gets a seat.

Route Clarity

Detailed stop schedules, pickup/drop timings, and driver emergency contacts available 24/7.
